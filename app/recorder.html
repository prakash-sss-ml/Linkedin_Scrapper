LinkedIn Job Recorder

Record the LinkedIn window or browser tab. When recording stops, the video is uploaded automatically and the extracted Excel file is downloaded.

Recording steps:
1. Click Start Recording.
2. Select the LinkedIn tab or browser window.
3. Open job posts and scroll slowly through each description.
4. Click Stop Recording or use the browser's Stop sharing button.
5. Wait for the Excel file to download automatically.
